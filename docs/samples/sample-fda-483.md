## INSPECTIONAL OBSERVATIONS

**OBSERVATION 1**

Your firm failed to establish and follow an adequate written testing program
designed to assess the stability characteristics of drug products. Specifically,
stability samples for Amoxicillin Capsules USP, Batch AMX-2025-114, were not
tested at the 6-month time station, and no deviation or investigation was
initiated to address the missed interval.

*Reference: 21 CFR 211.166(a)*

---

**OBSERVATION 2**

Laboratory records do not include a complete record of all data secured in the
course of each test. Chromatographic integration parameters in the HPLC data for
assay testing of Batch AMX-2025-114 were altered by an analyst on 18 February
2026 without documented justification, and the change was not subject to a
second-person review.

*Reference: 21 CFR 211.194(a)*

---

**OBSERVATION 3**

Equipment used in the manufacture, processing, and packing of drug products is
not cleaned and maintained at appropriate intervals to prevent contamination
that would alter the safety, identity, strength, quality, or purity of the drug
product. The cleaning log for Granulator GR-04 shows no recorded cleaning
between 12 March 2026 and 27 March 2026, during which three product batches were
processed.

*Reference: 21 CFR 211.67(a)*

---

**OBSERVATION 4**

Employees engaged in the manufacture, processing, packing, or holding of a drug
product do not have the training required to perform their assigned functions.
Two operators observed performing aseptic filling operations in Cleanroom B on
04 March 2026 did not have current gowning qualification records on file.

*Reference: 21 CFR 211.25(a)*

---

**OBSERVATION 5**

Written procedures are not established and followed for the cleaning and
maintenance of the environmental monitoring program in the controlled
manufacturing areas. Standard Operating Procedure EM-018 ("Environmental
Monitoring of Controlled Areas") was overdue for its scheduled periodic review by
more than 90 days at the time of inspection.

*Reference: 21 CFR 211.56(b)*
